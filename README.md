# My Favorite Series
 
## Title: Black Clover
- Genre: Action / Fantasy / Adventure
- Main Character: Asta
- Why I Like It:
  - It shows determination, teamwork, and courage.
  - I like the scene where Asta keeps training even without magic—it’s inspiring and full of hope.
  - But I don’t like how some people look down on him just because he was born without magic.
